## Goal

Design a 3D-printed chassis and suspension for the rover with four-wheel independent steering
(4WIS, one servo per corner) that can **spin in place** and **crab walk**, printed in sections on
a LulzBot Mini.

## Constraints

| Constraint | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Printer bed | 152 × 152 × 158 mm (LulzBot Mini) | stock spec |
| Design envelope | **140 × 140 × 145 mm** per part | chosen safety margin |
| Wheels | 75 mm Ø × 30 mm, 12 mm hex | in hand |
| Drive motors | JGA25-370, 25 mm Ø, one per wheel, steers with the wheel | in hand |
| Motor + hex length | **72 mm**, from hex mating face to encoder back | measured |
| Hex seat | mating face is **10 mm** in from the wheel's outer face | measured |
| Battery | 4S4P 18650, 144 × 65 × 36 mm (two layers of 8 cells) | in hand |
| Steering servos | MG996R candidate, one per corner | not yet bought |

## Decisions so far

1. **Steering range: ±45° commanded, ±50° mechanical clearance.** Spin in place needs
   `atan(wheelbase / track)` at each wheel (45° for a square layout). Crab walk is then available
   up to 45° diagonally. True 90° sideways crab was rejected: it needs the servo's full throw and
   a much larger clearance pocket per corner.
2. **Design rule: wheelbase ≤ track**, so the spin angle stays ≤ 45°.
3. **Corner architecture: suspension above the wheel, steering pod below** (approach 1).
   - Double wishbone arms and shock mount **above ~80 mm**, clear of the motor sweep.
   - The MG996R sits in the upright; the servo shaft is the kingpin, vertical, through the tire
     center (zero scrub).
   - A bearing in the upright carries the wheel load; the servo only supplies torque.
   - A yoke hangs from the kingpin, clamps the motor, and turns with the wheel.
   - Rejected: RC-style C-hub wishbone at axle height (the motor tail swings into the arms) and a
     walking-beam with no springs (simpler, but no real travel). The walking-beam could reuse the
     same steering pods later if needed.
4. **Battery crosswise, low, between the axles**, hung from two side cradles. No single print
   has to span its 144 mm length.
5. **Three chassis modules** (front, center, rear), each ≤140 mm, joined by two lengthwise rails
   (carbon tube or aluminum). Front and rear modules are the same part.

## Clearance study

Each tire + motor unit is swept through ±50° about its kingpin. With the measured hex seat, the
motor tail is **67 mm** inboard of the kingpin. Battery clearance to the nearest sweep:

| Track × wheelbase | Spin angle | Battery clearance / side |
|---|---|---|
| 240 × 240 | 45.0° | 39.2 mm |
| 240 × 230 | 43.8° | 34.2 mm |
| 240 × 220 | 42.5° | 29.2 mm |
| **220 × 220** | **45.0°** | **21.0 mm** |
| 220 × 210 | 43.7° | 16.0 mm |
| 220 × 200 | 42.3° | 11.0 mm |

Earlier estimates (motor assumed 95 mm, then 87 mm, from the kingpin) made the battery the
limiting factor and pointed to 240 × 240. With the measured hex seat, it no longer limits the
size.

## Recommendation: 220 × 220 track × wheelbase

- The smallest square layout with a comfortable (~20 mm) battery margin; spin in place is
  exactly 45° at every corner.
- **Arms ~55 mm lower / ~50 mm upper.** Inner pivots land at about ±55–60 mm from the centerline,
  leaving room for bosses inside the 140 mm module envelope. The 42 mm upper arm in
  `hardware.md` does not fit a one-piece module at either track width.
- Footprint about 295 × 250 mm, versus 315 × 270 mm at 240 × 240. Shorter rails and smaller
  modules save print time and weight.
- **Choose 240 × 240 instead** for rougher ground (rugs, thresholds, outdoors): longer arms
  (~60 / 55 mm) change camber less over travel, and the wider stance resists tipping.

The drawing currently shows 240 × 240; it will be regenerated at the chosen size.

## Open questions

- Track × wheelbase: 220 × 220 (recommended) or 240 × 240?
- Printer Z height: confirm against the actual machine (158 mm stock).
- Servo: confirm MG996R, and its real travel at the chosen pulse range.

## Next sections to design

1. **Corner module:** upright, bearing and thrust path, servo-to-kingpin coupling, yoke and
   motor clamp, and wiring through the steering joint (motor + encoder, 6 wires, ±50° twist).
2. **Chassis modules and print split:** module outlines within 140 × 140, rail interface,
   battery cradles, electronics deck.
3. **Suspension geometry:** arm lengths and pivot heights, shock mounting, 25 mm travel check.
4. **Validation:** extend `corner_layout_study.py` to check sweeps at full bump and full droop,
   plus a print-fit check of every part against the envelope.
5. **Firmware impact** (separate spec): `Steering` class, Ackermann / crab / spin modes, and four
   servo PWM pins (see `docs/pinouts.md`).
